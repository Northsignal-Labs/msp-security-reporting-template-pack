# NIS2 Readiness Checklist for MSP Clients

Northsignal Labs — free operational evidence checklist for MSPs and small IT teams preparing client conversations around NIS2 readiness.

> Important disclaimer: this is an operational checklist, not legal, audit, regulatory, or compliance advice. NIS2 obligations vary by country, entity type, sector, size, contracts, and local transposition. Use this only to organize evidence and questions for qualified legal/compliance review.

## Intended use

Use this checklist to run a low-risk readiness discovery session with an MSP client or internal stakeholder. The goal is to identify existing evidence, gaps, owners, and next actions without pretending that a template can determine legal applicability.

## Evidence checklist

### 1. Governance and risk management

- `[ ]` Named executive owner for cybersecurity risk
- `[ ]` Current information security policy or equivalent operating standard
- `[ ]` Cyber risk register with owners and review dates
- `[ ]` Management review cadence for security risks and incidents
- `[ ]` Supplier/customer contractual security obligations collected

### 2. Asset, service, and dependency mapping

- `[ ]` Inventory of critical business services
- `[ ]` Inventory of systems supporting each critical service
- `[ ]` Identity/account inventory for privileged access
- `[ ]` Third-party supplier and SaaS dependency list
- `[ ]` Backup and recovery dependency map

### 3. Identity and access controls

- `[ ]` MFA coverage report for users and administrators
- `[ ]` Privileged account review completed within the last quarter
- `[ ]` Joiner/mover/leaver process evidence
- `[ ]` Break-glass account controls and review dates
- `[ ]` Conditional access or equivalent access policy evidence

### 4. Vulnerability, patching, and configuration

- `[ ]` Patch compliance report for endpoints and servers
- `[ ]` Vulnerability scan or exposure review evidence, where available
- `[ ]` Secure configuration baseline for endpoints and cloud services
- `[ ]` Exception register for unsupported systems or delayed patches
- `[ ]` Remediation owner and target date for high-risk findings

### 5. Backup, continuity, and recovery

- `[ ]` Backup policy with retention and coverage scope
- `[ ]` Latest backup success/failure report
- `[ ]` Latest restore test evidence and lessons learned
- `[ ]` Recovery time/recovery point expectations documented
- `[ ]` Business continuity contacts and escalation path

### 6. Incident handling and reporting readiness

- `[ ]` Incident response plan or playbook
- `[ ]` Severity definitions and triage process
- `[ ]` Internal escalation contacts
- `[ ]` External advisor/regulator/customer notification decision path
- `[ ]` Incident log template and evidence retention location

### 7. Supplier and MSP operating model

- `[ ]` MSP responsibilities documented separately from client responsibilities
- `[ ]` Supplier security review checklist
- `[ ]` Critical supplier contact and escalation list
- `[ ]` Contract/security addendum evidence, if applicable
- `[ ]` Process for tracking supplier incidents and service outages

### 8. Awareness and training

- `[ ]` Security awareness or phishing training evidence
- `[ ]` Admin/operator training notes for incident and escalation processes
- `[ ]` Management briefing on operational cyber risk responsibilities
- `[ ]` Record of policy acceptance or staff communication
