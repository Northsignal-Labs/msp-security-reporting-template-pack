# Northsignal Labs publication checklist

Purpose: local pre-publication review checklist for releasing the Northsignal Labs MSP/cybersecurity template pack through a future approved, no-spend, non-personal publishing route. This file does not publish anything, create accounts, collect payments, or authorize use of any private individual's personal identity.

## Release gate

Do not publish externally until all required items are checked or explicitly marked not applicable by an approved human reviewer.

## 1. Identity and privacy

- [ ] Public-facing project name is **Northsignal Labs** only.
- [ ] No mention of any private individual's name, personal background, personal accounts, employer, location, or private contact details.
- [ ] No invented human founder, team member, customer, testimonial, certification, partnership, or revenue claim.
- [ ] If attribution is needed, wording is truthful: "Northsignal Labs is a small independent automation lab building practical cybersecurity/MSP templates and local tools."
- [ ] Repository/account/channel profile does not expose any private individual through username, avatar, email, commit metadata, billing, domain registration, or linked social accounts.

## 2. No-spend and no-financial-risk checks

- [ ] Publishing route is free-tier/static only; no domain purchase, paid hosting, paid marketplace, ads, boosts, subscriptions, or paid analytics.
- [ ] No credit card, paid trial, billing commitment, payout setup, tax/KYC workflow, or revenue-processing account is created autonomously.
- [ ] Affiliate/referral links are absent for first validation; do not add placeholders, disclosure-dependent links, partner-tracking, commissions, or sponsorship hooks without a separate explicit monetization approval.
- [ ] No customer-specific work, SLA, professional service promise, refund obligation, or support commitment is offered.

## 3. Content claims and disclaimers

- [ ] README and relevant pages include the operational-use notice: not legal, insurance, brokerage, compliance, audit, certification, Microsoft-affiliation, or professional-services advice.
- [ ] NIS2 material is framed as operational evidence collection, not legal/compliance advice.
- [ ] M365 Secure Score material does not imply Microsoft affiliation, licensing guidance, complete security assurance, or certification.
- [ ] Cyber-insurance material does not imply coverage, underwriting, claims, pricing, brokerage, or legal advice.
- [ ] vCISO/QBR material does not imply credentialed vCISO services, audit authority, certification, or client-specific risk acceptance.
- [ ] All example data is generic sample data and contains no real client, tenant, employee, or security-sensitive details.

## 4. Static repository/file review

- [ ] `README.md` explains the pack, safe-use framing, local usage, and no-spend guardrails.
- [ ] `RELEASE-MANIFEST.json` defines public include/exclude decisions and excludes generated cache/internal telemetry files.
- [ ] `index.html` links to all five first-batch assets.
- [ ] Each HTML landing page links to its Markdown asset and back to the pack/index where appropriate.
- [ ] Markdown assets are download-friendly and do not require forms or external services.
- [ ] `tools/m365_secure_score_report_generator.py` runs locally without network access or paid dependencies.
- [ ] `samples/`, `schemas/`, and `generated/` contain only safe sample artifacts.
- [ ] `PUBLISHING-ROUTES.md`, `PUBLICATION-CHECKLIST.md`, and `CHANGELOG.md` are present for reviewer context.

## 5. Links, tracking, and data handling

- [ ] No external forms, trackers, pixels, mailing-list embeds, or client-data collection are included by default.
- [ ] No analytics scripts, search-console tracking, referrer logs, page-view tracking, pixels, cookies, forms, newsletter embeds, private inboxes, or lead-capture workflows are activated for first validation.
- [ ] Outbound links are limited, useful, static/resource-oriented, and not affiliate/referral, sponsorship, payment, quote, invoice, partner-tracking, commission, or sales links.
- [ ] Download paths are static files only; no sensitive input is uploaded or processed remotely.

## 6. Distribution approval

- [ ] Chosen channel is approved for Northsignal Labs use and does not require using any private individual's personal public identity.
- [ ] Community posting, cold outreach, newsletter distribution, or direct contact is not performed without explicit approval.
- [ ] Release announcement language is transparent: small independent automation lab sharing free operational templates.
- [ ] The initial public release can be reverted/removed without financial penalty.
