Copy/paste evidence checklist

1. Governance and risk management

  • [ ] Named executive owner for cybersecurity risk
  • [ ] Current information security policy or operating standard
  • [ ] Cyber risk register with owners and review dates
  • [ ] Management review cadence for security risks and incidents
  • [ ] Supplier/customer contractual security obligations collected

2. Assets, services, dependencies

  • [ ] Inventory of critical business services
  • [ ] Systems supporting each critical service
  • [ ] Privileged identity/account inventory
  • [ ] Third-party supplier and SaaS dependency list
  • [ ] Backup and recovery dependency map

3. Identity and access controls

  • [ ] MFA coverage report
  • [ ] Privileged account review completed within the last quarter
  • [ ] Joiner/mover/leaver evidence
  • [ ] Break-glass controls and review dates
  • [ ] Conditional access or equivalent policy evidence

4. Vulnerability and patching

  • [ ] Patch compliance report
  • [ ] Vulnerability scan or exposure review evidence
  • [ ] Secure configuration baseline
  • [ ] Exception register for unsupported systems or delayed patches
  • [ ] Remediation owner and target date for high-risk findings

5. Backup and recovery

  • [ ] Backup policy with retention and scope
  • [ ] Latest backup success/failure report
  • [ ] Latest restore test evidence
  • [ ] RTO/RPO expectations documented
  • [ ] Continuity contacts and escalation path

6. Incident readiness

  • [ ] Incident response plan or playbook
  • [ ] Severity definitions and triage process
  • [ ] Internal escalation contacts
  • [ ] External notification decision path
  • [ ] Incident log template and evidence retention location

7. Supplier and MSP model

  • [ ] MSP vs client responsibility split
  • [ ] Supplier security review checklist
  • [ ] Critical supplier contact list
  • [ ] Contract/security addendum evidence, if applicable
  • [ ] Supplier incident and outage tracking process

8. Awareness and training

  • [ ] Security awareness or phishing training evidence
  • [ ] Admin/operator incident training notes
  • [ ] Management briefing on cyber risk responsibilities
  • [ ] Record of policy acceptance or staff communication
