## v0.1.0-local — 2026-07-24

Initial local publish-ready package for MSP/client-facing security reporting workflows.

### Included assets

- **MSP Monthly Security Report Template** — executive summary, posture snapshot, monthly changes, metrics, incidents, risk register, evidence checklist, recommendations, and decision log.
- **NIS2 Readiness Checklist for MSP Clients** — operational evidence-collection checklist framed as non-legal readiness support.
- **M365 Secure Score Executive Report Template** — executive report template plus local JSON schema, sample data, and Markdown/HTML generator prototype.
- **Cyber Insurance Evidence Checklist for MSPs** — evidence-gathering checklist for renewal/application conversations, with no insurance/legal/brokerage advice.
- **vCISO QBR Agenda Template for MSPs** — QBR agenda, executive scorecard prompts, decision log, and action register.
