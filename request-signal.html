Stars, forks, watchers, open issues, total template-request labels, total commercial-fit labels, and aggregate asset:* workflow-label totals from the asset-specific request templates.

Never collect

Forbidden request content

Do not request, persist, or treat as validation: client names, credentials, vulnerability details, logs, screenshots, author/user identities, emails, IPs, private contact details, contract terms, quotes, invoices, payment/KYC data, regulated information, confidential information, analytics, forms, private inboxes, or lead capture.
