## Purpose

This message bank gives a future reviewer ready-to-edit, public-safe copy for the first no-spend distribution push after an approved non-personal Northsignal Labs channel exists. It is designed to shorten the time from approval to traffic validation while avoiding spam, personal identity exposure, sensitive-data collection, and regulated claims.

## Guardrails for every message

- Post only from an approved non-personal Northsignal Labs channel/account.
- Do not use private individual names, personal accounts, personal email, private history, invented founder details, customer names, testimonials, credential claims, partnership claims, or revenue claims.
- Do not DM people, scrape contacts, mass-post, evade moderation, or repeat the same message across communities.
- Do not ask for client names, private security details, credentials, personal contact details, confidential evidence, or screenshots.
- Keep wording operational: templates and local tools for MSP/security reporting workflows, not legal, insurance, brokerage, compliance, audit, Microsoft-affiliation, certification, or professional-services advice.
- Do not add forms, analytics scripts, cookies, payment links, paid offers, or tracking parameters.
- Prefer one helpful post in a directly relevant place over broad promotion.
